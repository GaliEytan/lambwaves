\begin{abstract}
Frequency-domain models of laser-generated Lamb waves describe the asymptotic
harmonic response but do not determine how rapidly that response is established.
We formulate the switched-on thermoelastic excitation of an isotropic plate as a
causal modal problem. The formulation separates the steady harmonic field from
the transient contribution and exposes the roles of group velocity, dispersion
curvature, attenuation, source width, and observation distance. It also provides
a common framework for finite bursts and for moving or spatially periodic laser
sources. Particular attention is paid to zero-group-velocity points, where a
propagation-time estimate fails and damping controls resonant buildup. The aim is
to turn transient formation into experimentally useful predictions of required
burst length and time-dependent modal purity.
\end{abstract}
