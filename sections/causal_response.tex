\section{Causal buildup of the harmonic response}

Let $a_n(k,t)$ denote a normalized modal coordinate. A minimal damped model is
\begin{equation}
 \ddot a_n+2\gamma_n(k)\dot a_n+\omega_n^2(k)a_n=F_n(k,t),
 \qquad a_n(k,0)=\dot a_n(k,0)=0.
 \label{eq:modal-oscillator}
\end{equation}
For the source in Eq.~\eqref{eq:laser-source}, causal convolution with the modal
Green function yields
\begin{equation}
 u(x,z,t)=\sum_n\int\frac{\dd k}{2\pi}\,
 \bm\phi_n(z;k)\ee^{\ii kx}
 \int_0^t G_n(k,t-t')F_n(k,t')\,\dd t'.
 \label{eq:causal-response}
\end{equation}
The time integral can be separated exactly into a forced harmonic term and terms
at the damped natural frequency:
\begin{equation}
  u_n=u_{n,\mathrm{ss}}+u_{n,\mathrm{tr}}.
\end{equation}
We define a practical settling time by
\begin{equation}
 t_{\mathrm{ss},n}(x,\epsilon)=
 \inf\left\{t:\frac{|u_{n,\mathrm{tr}}(x,t')|}
 {|u_{n,\mathrm{ss}}(x,t')|}<\epsilon
 \ \text{for all }t'>t\right\}.
 \label{eq:settling-time}
\end{equation}
Zeros of the denominator and beat nodes require an envelope- or cycle-averaged
version of Eq.~\eqref{eq:settling-time} in numerical work.

Away from special points, the transient phase is
\begin{equation}
 \Phi(k)=kx-\omega_n(k)t,
\end{equation}
and stationary contributions satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \omega_n'(k)=\frac{x}{t}.
 \label{eq:stationary-phase}
\end{equation}
Consequently the decay depends on $\omega_n''$, higher derivatives at degenerate
points, and the source spectrum---not solely on a single group velocity.

At exact ZGV resonance, Eq.~\eqref{eq:zgv-expansion} gives an impulse response
proportional to $t^{-1/2}\ee^{-\ii\omega_0t}$ for a generic one-dimensional
spectral integral. Continuous resonant forcing therefore produces idealized
$\sqrt{t}$ growth. Attenuation changes the kernel to
$t^{-1/2}\ee^{-\gamma t}$ and restores a finite asymptote on a scale of order
$1/\gamma$. This separates propagation-controlled and resonance-controlled
buildup.

For a finite $N$-cycle burst, the same convolution defines
\begin{equation}
 N_{95}=\min\left\{N:\max_t|u_n^{(N)}(t)|
 \geq0.95\,|u_{n,\mathrm{CW}}|\right\},
\end{equation}
with the comparison metric to be refined for dispersive wave packets. A second
observable is the time-dependent modal purity
\begin{equation}
 P_m(t)=\frac{|A_m(t)|^2}{\sum_n|A_n(t)|^2}.
\end{equation}
