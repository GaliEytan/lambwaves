\section{Moving and periodic laser sources}

For a source translated at speed $V$,
\begin{equation}
 Q(x,t)=q(x-Vt)g(t),
\end{equation}
the space--time transform contains the constraint
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde Q(k,\omega)\propto\widetilde q(k)\,
 \widetilde g(\omega-kV).
\end{equation}
For steady translation this concentrates spectral weight along $\omega=kV$.
Intersections of this line with $\omega_n(k)$ identify phase-matched Lamb modes.

A spatial array with pitch $\Lambda$ supplies reciprocal wavenumbers
\begin{equation}
 k_m=k_c+\frac{2\pi m}{\Lambda},\qquad m\in\mathbb Z,
\end{equation}
where $k_c$ represents any carrier imposed by scanning or phase delays. Combining
translation, periodicity, and temporal modulation permits selective excitation by
requiring simultaneous spectral overlap, dispersion matching, and a large modal
overlap in Eq.~\eqref{eq:modal-overlap}. The causal formulation additionally asks
how quickly that selectivity is established after the source begins.
