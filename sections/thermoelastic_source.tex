\section{Thermoelastic laser forcing}

We write a switched-on modulated source as
\begin{equation}
 Q(x,z,t)=\Heaviside(t)\,Q_0 f(x)a(z)\cos(\Omega t),
 \label{eq:laser-source}
\end{equation}
where $f$ is the beam profile and $a$ describes optical absorption through the
thickness. A Gaussian line source of $1/e$ half-width $w$ has
\begin{equation}
  f(x)=\exp(-x^2/w^2),\qquad
  \widetilde f(k)=\sqrt{\pi}w\exp(-k^2w^2/4).
\end{equation}
Thus reducing $w$ broadens the accessible wavenumber range.

After solving the heat equation, the generalized force on mode $n$ can be
written schematically as
\begin{equation}
  F_n(k,t)=\int_{-h}^{h}
  \bm\phi_n^{\dagger}(z;k)\cdot
  \bm f_{\mathrm{th}}(k,z,t)\,\dd z.
  \label{eq:modal-overlap}
\end{equation}
This overlap is essential: intersection of the source spectrum with a dispersion
branch shows that a mode is available, not that it is strongly excited.
