\chapter*{Notation and conventions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Notation and conventions}
The plate occupies $-h\le z\le h$ and has thickness $d=2h$. Repeated Latin
indices imply summation. Bold symbols are vectors; a dot is a time derivative
and ${}^*$ complex conjugation. The Fourier convention is
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde f(k,\omega)=\iint f(x,t)\ee^{-\ii(kx-\omega t)}\dd x\dd t,
 \quad f(x,t)=\iint\frac{\widetilde f(k,\omega)}{(2\pi)^2}
 \ee^{\ii(kx-\omega t)}\dd k\dd\omega.\label{eq:fourier-convention}
\end{equation}
Thus positive-frequency phasors multiply $\ee^{-\ii\omega t}$. Stress is
positive in tension. Absorbed power density $Q$ has units \si{W.m^{-3}}.
