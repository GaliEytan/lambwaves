\chapter{Thermoelastic modal projection}\label{app:projection}
Let $\tau_{ij}=(3\lambda+2\mu)\alpha\Delta T\delta_{ij}$. The strong equation is
$\rho\ddot u_i-(C_{ijkl}u_{k,l})_{,j}=-\tau_{ij,j}$. Multiplication by $U_i^*$
and integration gives a volume term $\int U_{i,j}^*\tau_{ij}\dd z$ plus the
boundary term $-[U_i^*\tau_{ij}n_j]$. If elastic traction alone is prescribed,
that surface term is an applied thermal traction. If total traction is zero, it
cancels the boundary term from integration of elastic stress, leaving
\cref{eq:weak-thermal}. This bookkeeping is why a truncated body-force density
cannot be used without its surface distribution.

With $M_n=1$ and $\tau_{ij}=\tau\delta_{ij}$,
\begin{equation}F_n=\int_{-h}^{h}\tau(k,z,t)
[-\ii kU_{n,x}^*(z;k)+\partial_zU_{n,z}^*(z;k)]\dd z.
\end{equation}
The bracket is the conjugate modal dilatation under the adopted Fourier sign.
