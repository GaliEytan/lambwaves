\chapter{Boundary determinant and eigenfield algebra}\label{app:boundary}
For the S ansatz, substitution at $z=h$ gives the two rows of
\cref{eq:matrix-S}. Its determinant divided by $\mu^2$ is
\begin{equation}
4k^2pq\sin(ph)\cos(qh)+(q^2-k^2)^2\cos(ph)\sin(qh)=0.
\end{equation}
For A modes,
\begin{equation}
4k^2pq\cos(ph)\sin(qh)+(q^2-k^2)^2\sin(ph)\cos(qh)=0.
\end{equation}
Dividing produces \cref{eq:RL-S} and \cref{eq:RL-A} only when the divided factors are
nonzero. A stable eigenvector may be taken from the larger-norm matrix row: for
example, solve $D/A=-M_{11}/M_{12}$ when $|M_{12}|$ is safe, otherwise use the
second row. Insert the ratio into \cref{eq:eigen-u} and scale by
\cref{eq:modal-mass}. Phase is fixed by requiring the largest sampled displacement
component to be real and positive, enabling continuity comparisons.
